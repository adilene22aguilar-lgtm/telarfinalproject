******Welcome to The Preservation of Writing and Power**** **
On this page, we explore the theme of "How Writing Preserves and Transforms Power Across Societies" as we describe the changes in writing that have occurred over time across various societies. We focus on the societies of the Incas and the Aztecs/Nahua, using illustrations from the Florentine Codex and the Spanish invasion of Peru, as explored in Nueva Crónica y Buen Gobierno by Felipe Guamán Poma de Ayala. We also examine Simon Rodriguez's belief in the importance of education and writing, which highlights how they serve as powerful tools for empowerment, personal growth, and the development of future generations.
