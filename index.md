******Welcome to The Preservation of Writing and Power**** **
On this page, we explore the theme of "How Writing Preserves and Transforms Power Across Societies" as we describe the changes in writing that have changed over time across various societies. We focus on the societies of the Incas and the Aztecs/Nahua, using illustrations from the Florentine Codex and the Spanish invasion of Peru, explored in Nueva Coronica y Buen Gobierno by Felipe Guamán Poma de Ayala. In addition, Simon Rodriguez's belief in the significance of education in writing highlights how it serves as a powerful tool for empowerment, personal growth, and the development of future generations.
